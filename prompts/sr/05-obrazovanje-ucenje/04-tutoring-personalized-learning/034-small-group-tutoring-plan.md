# PLAN TUTORSTVA U MALOJ GRUPI

Glavni cilj:

> Izgradite "Plan tutorstva u maloj grupi" kao dijagnostički i adaptivni tutorski workflow koji čuva visoka očekivanja i napreduje prema samostalnosti učenika.

## 1. DIJAGNOZA PRE INSTRUKCIJE

Capture the target, prerequisite knowledge, representative errors, learner explanation, confidence, recent performance and what support is currently needed.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- group by current learning need rather than fixed labels
- define shared target
- ensure every learner responds
- use peer interaction purposefully
- monitor individual understanding
- regroup when evidence changes

## 3. MASTERY MODEL

Use statuses:
- SECURE
- FRAGILE
- PARTIAL
- MISSING
- MISCONCEIVED
- NOT YET TESTED

Do not mark mastery from one successful heavily scaffolded attempt.

## 4. HINT LADDER

Prefer:
1. ask learner to restate problem
2. activate prior knowledge
3. point to relevant feature
4. give strategic prompt
5. provide partial step
6. model only when necessary
7. immediately return responsibility to learner

## 5. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Mastery Matrix
| Concept / skill | Status | Evidence | Error pattern | Next step | Recheck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Support-Fading Matrix
| Task | Current support | Independence target | Fade trigger | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Progress Matrix
| Objective | Baseline | Current | Delayed retention | Transfer | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES

Avoid answer-giving, fixed learner labels, over-scaffolding, preference-only personalization, endless remediation with no recheck, acceleration without prerequisites and progress metrics that measure activity instead of learning.

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Learner diagnosis.
2. Priority gap.
3. Tutoring sequence.
4. Question/hint ladder.
5. Guided and independent practice.
6. Mastery evidence.
7. Support-fading plan.
8. Delayed retention and transfer check.

End with **Provera integriteta tutorstva** confirming that tutoring responds to evidence and increases learner independence rather than dependence on the tutor.

Ovaj prompt podržava tutorstvo i ne zamenjuje stručni sud nastavnika ili specijaliste za učenje kada je potreban.
