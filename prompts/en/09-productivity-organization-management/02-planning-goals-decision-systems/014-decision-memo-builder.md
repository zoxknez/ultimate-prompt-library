# DECISION MEMO BUILDER

Main objective:

> Perform an executable, auditable workflow for Decision Memo Builder, with a clear objective, owner, assumptions, dependencies, risks and review triggers.

## 1. OPERATING CONTEXT
Define objective, scope, owner, stakeholders, constraints, deadline, dependencies, current state, available capacity and success criteria.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD
Planning should convert intent into decision criteria, explicit trade-offs, milestones and review cadence. Goals are useful only when they change allocation of time/resources and have evidence of progress.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS
- goal has measurable outcome
- owner is explicit
- assumptions are logged
- decision criteria are defined before choosing
- roadmap reflects dependencies
- scenario plans define triggers
- OKRs avoid activity-only key results
- review cadence can change or stop the plan

## 4. OPERATING CARD
```text
Objective:
Owner:
Scope:
Dependency:
Assumption:
Risk:
Decision:
Next action:
Deadline/trigger:
Success evidence:
Review date:
```

## 5. REQUIRED MATRICES
### Goal/Decision Matrix
| Objective | Measure | Owner | Assumption | Dependency | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Decision Matrix
| Option | Criteria | Evidence | Risk | Reversibility | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid ownerless work, hidden dependencies, vague goals, unbounded scope, excessive process, automation without controls, status reporting with no decision value and plans that ignore capacity.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT
1. Objective and scope.
2. Owner/stakeholders.
3. Dependencies and assumptions.
4. Risks/controls.
5. Required matrices.
6. Execution sequence.
7. Review cadence.
8. Stop/change/escalation triggers.

End with **Operating Integrity Check** confirming every active item has ownership, a next action and a review mechanism.
