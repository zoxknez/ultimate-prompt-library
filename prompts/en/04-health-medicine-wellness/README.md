Health literacy, medicine, nutrition, physical activity, mental well-being and healthcare work.

## What this category covers

- Health literacy and understanding medical information
- Nutrition, fitness and healthy habits
- Mental well-being and stress management
- Workflows for healthcare professionals

> Prompts in this category support understanding and preparation. They do not replace diagnosis or treatment by a qualified health professional.

## How to contribute

- Open a **prompt suggestion** issue describing the prompt you would like to see or write. Check the [catalog](../../../catalog.json) and existing issues first to avoid duplicates.
- Prompt IDs for this category use the `UPL-HEALTH-NNN` prefix and are assigned only after a proposal is accepted.
- New prompts should follow the [prompt format guide](../../../docs/prompt-format.md) and ship in English and Serbian before they are marked `stable`.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full process.
