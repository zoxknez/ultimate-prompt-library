# INFLATION & COST PRESSURE ANALYSIS

I want a deep analysis of inflation and cost pressure focused on business transmission, pricing power, margins, wages, inputs and real purchasing power.

Main objective:

> Determine which part of the inflation pressure affects the business or sector in question, how quickly it passes through the cost base and how much can be passed on to customers without an unacceptable loss of volume.

This is not:

- an analysis of headline CPI only
- an assumption that CPI = company cost inflation
- an assumption that inflation down means costs down
- automatically linking every price increase to inflation

## 44. EVIDENCE TIERS

```text
A - verified company/input price data
B - official relevant price/wage indices
C - strong sector evidence
D - inferred pass-through
E - scenario
```

Materiality is the severity scale of this analysis: **HIGH** changes the overall conclusion or the decision; **MEDIUM** changes a key metric, driver or trend but not the conclusion; **LOW** has a limited effect and is noted for completeness. Judge it against an explicit base (gross margin, EBITDA or cash) and state that base. A finding is CONFIRMED only with evidence tier A or B; tier C is LIKELY; tier D stays NOT VERIFIED, and tier E is a SCENARIO. Never present an estimate, a forecast or a scenario as a fact.
