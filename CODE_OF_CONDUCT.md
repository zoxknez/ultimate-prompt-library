# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Ultimate Prompt Library to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- showing empathy and kindness toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private;
- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances;
- trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews - and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainers privately - for example through the contact options on the maintainers' GitHub profiles. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy of the reporter.
