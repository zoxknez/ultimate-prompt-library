# STRESS MANAGEMENT EVIDENCE PLAN

Main objective:

> Build an evidence-based stress-management plan that distinguishes normal stress from clinically significant impairment.

## 1. CRISIS GATE

Immediately prioritize local emergency/crisis support if there is:
- current intent or plan to self-harm or die
- recent serious self-harm / suicide attempt
- immediate danger to another person
- severe agitation, inability to maintain basic safety or rapidly escalating behavior
- severe intoxication/withdrawal with medical danger
- severe confusion, catatonia, inability to care for basic needs or other acute emergency

Do not bury crisis guidance after a long analysis.

## 2. CONTEXT & FUNCTION

Capture symptom type, onset, duration, severity, sleep, appetite, concentration, daily function, work/school impact, relationships, substance use, medications, prior episodes, treatment history, supports and current safety.

## 3. EVIDENCE-BASED SUPPORT

Use current authoritative mental-health guidance such as WHO mhGAP where relevant. Distinguish:
- psychoeducation
- self-management support
- structured psychological interventions
- clinician-delivered treatment
- medication discussion with prescriber
- urgent/crisis care

Never present a screening questionnaire as a diagnosis.

## 4. DO NOT REINFORCE AVOIDANCE / DELUSIONS

Do not validate unverified paranoid, delusional or catastrophic interpretations as fact. Do not design avoidance rituals that reinforce anxiety. Validate distress while keeping factual claims neutral and reality-based.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Crisis/safety assessment first.
2. Neutral symptom and function summary.
3. Evidence-based support options.
4. Care-navigation options.
5. Practical next steps.
6. What needs professional assessment.
7. Explicit escalation triggers.
8. Follow-up / appointment preparation.

End with **Mental Health Safety Check** confirming that crisis risk was handled first, no diagnosis was asserted, and no harmful belief or avoidance pattern was reinforced.

This does not replace a qualified mental-health professional or emergency/crisis care.
