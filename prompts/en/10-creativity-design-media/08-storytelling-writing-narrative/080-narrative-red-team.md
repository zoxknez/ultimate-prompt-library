# NARRATIVE RED TEAM

Main objective:

> Perform a production-grade workflow for Narrative Red Team, with clear intent, quality criteria, revision loops and traceable delivery.

## 1. CONTEXT
Define audience, objective, medium, length/format, tone, references, constraints, rights, accessibility, stakeholders, deadline and approval owner.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD
Narrative work should define dramatic/narrative function, point of view, stakes, progression and tone. Scenes, characters and worldbuilding should earn their place in the whole rather than exist as isolated decoration.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS
- central dramatic question is clear
- character wants/constraints are coherent
- scene has a function
- dialogue carries subtext where appropriate
- world rules are consistent
- tone is stable
- pacing matches narrative intent
- adaptation preserves function rather than literal surface

## 4. REVIEW CARD

## 5. REQUIRED MATRICES
### Narrative Matrix
| Beat/scene | Purpose | Character goal | Conflict | Change | Setup/payoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Revision Matrix
| Issue | Evidence | Structural? | Priority | Revision | Recheck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid revision by taste alone, narrative elements without function, feedback with no priority, version confusion, unclear approval authority, inaccessible outputs and delivery without preflight.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT
1. Intent and audience.
2. Structure/system.
3. Quality findings.
4. Required matrices.
5. Revision priorities.
6. Acceptance criteria.
7. Delivery/version plan.
8. Final integrity check.

End with **Final Creative Integrity Check** confirming the artifact is coherent, original, accessible, approved and ready for delivery.
