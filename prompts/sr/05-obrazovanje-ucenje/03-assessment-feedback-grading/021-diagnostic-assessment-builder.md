# IZRADA DIJAGNOSTIČKE PROCENE

Glavni cilj:

> Dizajnirajte ili auditujte "Izrada dijagnostičke procene" tako da procena meri nameravani konstrukt, generiše pouzdane dokaze i vodi ka konkretnoj nastavnoj ili učeničkoj akciji.

## 1. SVRHA PROCENE

Define whether the purpose is diagnostic, formative, summative, certification, selection or progress monitoring. Do not combine purposes without checking the consequences.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- define diagnostic purpose
- sample prerequisite knowledge and misconceptions
- keep stakes low
- use discriminating items
- map each response pattern to instructional action
- avoid converting diagnosis into grades

## 3. VALIDITY / RELIABILITY / FAIRNESS

Check:
- construct alignment
- content coverage
- scoring consistency
- accessibility
- accommodations
- bias and construct-irrelevant difficulty
- consequences of errors
- whether evidence is strong enough for the decision stakes

## 4. EVIDENCE LOOP

```text
Objective:
Assessment evidence:
Observed response:
Likely interpretation:
Alternative explanation:
Confidence:
Action:
Recheck:
```

## 5. FEEDBACK QUALITY

Effective feedback should clarify the learning goal, identify the most important gap and prompt the learner to think or act. Avoid doing all correction for the learner or giving so much feedback that none is used.

## 6. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Assessment Blueprint
| Objective | Importance | Task/item | Cognitive demand | Evidence | Scoring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Evidence-to-Action Matrix
| Finding | Interpretation | Confidence | Teacher action | Learner action | Recheck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Fairness Matrix
| Potential barrier | Construct relevance | Group impact | Accommodation | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Purpose and stakes.
2. Construct / objective map.
3. Assessment blueprint.
4. Quality / fairness findings.
5. Scoring or feedback plan.
6. Evidence-to-action loop.
7. Moderation / reliability controls.
8. Recheck plan.

End with **Provera integriteta procene** confirming that every score or judgment is tied to the intended learning construct and sufficient evidence.

Ovaj prompt podržava obrazovno procenjivanje i ne zamenjuje lokalna pravila ocenjivanja, akreditacije ili pristupačnosti.
