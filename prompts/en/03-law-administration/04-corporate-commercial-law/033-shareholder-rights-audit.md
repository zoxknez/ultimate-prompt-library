# SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS AUDIT

Main objective:

> Audit voting, information, economic, minority, transfer, pre-emption, meeting and enforcement rights across law, charter, bylaws and shareholder agreements.

Use a jurisdiction-aware, evidence-first method. Treat governance frameworks such as the G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance as comparative best-practice references only where useful, never as a substitute for applicable law, constitutional documents or transaction-specific obligations.

## 1. INTAKE GATE

Establish:
- entity type, jurisdiction and registration details
- ownership and control structure
- constitutional documents and shareholder agreements
- board / management structure and delegated authorities
- transaction or decision under review
- regulated status and sector-specific rules
- material filings, registers and corporate records
- relevant dates, approvals and deadlines
- known conflicts, disputes or related parties
- cross-border entities and beneficial owners

## 2. EVIDENCE HIERARCHY

Prioritize:
1. legislation and official registries
2. constitutional documents and filed instruments
3. executed shareholder / investment / JV agreements
4. signed resolutions, minutes and written consents
5. cap tables and ownership ledgers reconciled to primary records
6. regulator or exchange rules where applicable
7. internal policies and delegations
8. management statements and unverified summaries

Mark each conclusion VERIFIED / SUPPORTED / CONTESTED / UNVERIFIED / NOT APPLICABLE.

## 3. CORE ANALYSIS

For every material issue identify:
- legal rule or contractual source
- decision-maker and approval threshold
- quorum / voting / consent mechanics
- conflict or independence issue
- notice / meeting / record requirement
- filing or disclosure requirement
- economic or control consequence
- remedy or cure path
- evidence supporting compliance
- missing evidence and deadline risk

## 4. GOVERNANCE FAILURE MODES

Actively test for:
- action taken by the wrong body or without authority
- inconsistent charter, bylaws and shareholder agreement
- hidden veto or reserved-matter rights
- conflict-of-interest process failures
- undocumented delegation
- cap-table / registry mismatch
- stale beneficial-ownership data
- defective notice, quorum or voting
- unsigned or backdated records
- change-of-control clauses triggered indirectly
- missing filings or approvals
- governance rights that are practically unenforceable

## 5. SPECIALIZED QUESTIONS

For **Shareholder Rights Audit**, specifically:
- define the legal and commercial question precisely
- identify all actors whose rights or duties are affected
- reconstruct the decision and evidence chain chronologically
- test both formal validity and practical enforceability
- identify minority / creditor / regulator / counterparty consequences where relevant
- separate mandatory law from contractual governance and best practice
- surface cross-document inconsistencies
- produce cure steps in correct legal sequence


Severity:
P0 - invalid authority, ownership or legality defect capable of voiding or blocking the action
P1 - material governance/control defect or undisclosed conflict
P2 - significant approval, disclosure, record or transaction risk
P3 - correctable formal defect or evidence gap
P4 - governance hardening or best-practice improvement

## 8. REQUIRED OUTPUT

Return:
1. Executive governance / transaction summary.
2. Entity and ownership map.
3. Applicable legal and contractual framework.
4. Material findings P0-P4.
5. Required matrices.
6. Approval and filing sequence.
7. Conflicts and related-party analysis.
8. Missing records / diligence requests.
9. Cure plan with owners and deadlines.
10. Final validity and enforceability check.

End with **Corporate Integrity Check** confirming that every material ownership, authority, approval and filing conclusion is tied to primary evidence or explicitly marked unresolved.

This supports legal research and corporate preparation. It does not replace qualified legal advice in the relevant jurisdiction.
