# DIZAJN WORKED EXAMPLE PRIMERA

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite evidence-informed i context-aware workflow za "Dizajn worked example primera", sa jasnim ciljevima učenja, prethodnim znanjem, kontrolom kognitivnog opterećenja, praksom i proverom da li je učenje stvarno nastalo.

## 1. KONTEKST UČENJA

Establish:
- learner age / stage and subject
- prior knowledge and likely misconceptions
- target knowledge / skill / transfer
- available time and instructional setting
- language and accessibility needs
- assessment constraints
- resources / technology
- what learners must remember later, not only perform immediately

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- choose a representative problem
- make expert reasoning visible
- highlight decision points and common errors
- pair examples with completion problems
- vary examples to support transfer
- fade worked steps as competence increases

## 3. EVIDENCE-INFORMED GATE

Use high-quality syntheses and guidance as evidence-informed "best bets", not universal guarantees. Explicitly consider local context, teacher expertise, learner needs and implementation quality. Where evidence is mixed, state the uncertainty rather than forcing a method.

## 4. MODEL UČENJA

For each major activity:
```text
Learning objective:
Prior knowledge required:
Misconception risk:
Teacher input:
Learner thinking:
Practice type:
Feedback:
Cognitive-load risk:
Retrieval / review:
Evidence of learning:
Transfer check:
Adaptation trigger:
```

## 5. ANTI-ILLUSION TEST

Actively check for:
- engagement mistaken for learning
- fluency mistaken for durable knowledge
- copying mistaken for independent performance
- immediate success mistaken for long-term retention
- group completion hiding individual gaps
- attractive multimedia adding distraction
- task difficulty caused by unclear instructions rather than productive challenge
- activities not aligned to the objective
- assessment that samples the wrong construct

## 6. METAKOGNICIJA

Where appropriate, teach learners to:
- plan how to approach the task
- monitor whether the strategy is working
- evaluate the result
- explain why a strategy fits the task
- gradually internalize prompts as scaffolds are removed

## 7. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Alignment Matrix
| Objective | Activity | Practice | Feedback | Assessment | Alignment risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Cognitive Demand Matrix
| Step | New elements | Prior knowledge | Support | Load risk | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Learning Evidence Matrix
| Claim of learning | Evidence | Immediate/delayed | Individual/group | Retention | Transfer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 8. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Ciljeve i kontekst.
2. Dijagnostiku prethodnog znanja.
3. Evidence-informed dizajn.
4. Misconceptions i cognitive-load rizike.
5. Plan prakse i feedbacka.
6. Obavezne matrice.
7. Plan retention i transfer provere.
8. Adaptation triggers.

End with **Provera integriteta učenja** confirming that activities, practice and assessment all serve explicit learning goals and that performance is not being mistaken for durable learning.

Ovaj prompt podržava profesionalno planiranje nastave i ne zamenjuje stručni sud nastavnika niti lokalni kurikulum i pravila ustanove.
