# IZRADA PAID MEDIA STRATEGIJE

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite "Izrada paid media strategije" kroz jasnu hipotezu, ekonomiku, eksperimentalni dizajn, attribution ograničenja i odluke o skaliranju.

## 1. PAID CONTEXT
Define objective, audience, offer, channel, budget, conversion event, attribution window, unit economics, creative inventory and landing experience.

## 2. PAID STANDARD
- optimize to the deepest reliable business event available
- separate platform attribution from incrementality
- define test hypothesis before spend
- isolate major variables where practical
- include creative, audience and landing-page interaction
- scale only after economics and operational capacity support it
- use holdouts/geo/tests where appropriate for incrementality

## 5. FAILURE MODES
Avoid optimizing to cheap but low-quality conversions, changing many variables at once, reading platform attribution as causality, scaling before product/sales capacity and declaring winners from noisy short tests.

## 6. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT
1. Objective/economics.
2. Channel/test strategy.
3. Creative/landing hypotheses.
4. Attribution assumptions.
5. Required matrices.
6. Scale/stop rules.
7. Risk/guardrails.
8. Learning agenda.

End with **Paid acquisition integritet provera** confirming decisions are tied to economics and test evidence, not platform vanity metrics.
