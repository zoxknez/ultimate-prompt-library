## 61. EVIDENCE TIERS

```text
A - primary industry/company operating data
B - multiple reliable sector sources
C - strong economic derivation
D - interpretation
E - scenario
```

Materiality is the severity scale of this analysis: **HIGH** changes the overall conclusion or the decision; **MEDIUM** changes a key metric, driver or trend but not the conclusion; **LOW** has a limited effect and is noted for completeness. Judge it against an explicit base (industry revenue, the profit pool or ROIC) and state that base. A finding is CONFIRMED only with evidence tier A or B; tier C is LIKELY; tier D stays NOT VERIFIED, and tier E is a SCENARIO. Never present an estimate, a forecast or a scenario as a fact.
