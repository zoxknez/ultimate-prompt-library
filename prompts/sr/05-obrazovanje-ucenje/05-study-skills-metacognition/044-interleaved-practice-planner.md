# PLANER INTERLEAVED PRACTICE UČENJA

Glavni cilj:

> Izgradite evidence-informed "Planer interleaved practice učenja" koji povećava samoregulaciju, trajno pamćenje i samostalnost umesto oslanjanja na pasivno ponavljanje.

## 1. STUDY CONTEXT

Record goal, deadline, prior knowledge, available time, course demands, current strategy, known weak areas, sleep/work constraints and assessment format.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- choose confusable or related topics
- alternate them purposefully
- require discrimination between methods
- avoid random topic switching
- use cumulative practice
- measure transfer

## 3. METAKOGNITIVNA PETLJA

Plan -> Monitor -> Evaluate -> Adjust.

Learners should answer:
- What am I trying to learn?
- What strategy fits this task?
- How will I know I learned it?
- What evidence shows a gap?
- What should I change next time?

## 4. ANTI-ILLUSION RULES

Do not treat rereading, highlighting, recognition, completed hours or confidence as sufficient evidence of learning. Prefer retrieval, explanation, application, delayed recall and transfer.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Error Log

### Retention Matrix

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Learning goal and constraints.
2. Study strategy.
3. Retrieval / spacing plan.
4. Error-tracking system.
5. Metacognitive prompts.
6. Delayed retention check.
7. Adaptation rules.
8. Sustainable routine.

End with **Provera integriteta učenja** confirming that progress is measured by independent retrieval and application, not time spent or familiarity.

Ovaj prompt podržava veštine učenja i ne zamenjuje stručnu podršku za specifične teškoće u učenju.
