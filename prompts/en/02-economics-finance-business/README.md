# Economics, Finance & Business

[Library home](../../../README.md) · [Roadmap](../../../docs/roadmap.md) · [Contributing](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md)

**Category ID:** `UPL-BIZ` · **Prompt IDs:** `UPL-BIZ-001`, `UPL-BIZ-002`, …

Economics, personal and corporate finance, accounting, strategy, entrepreneurship and business operations.

The first 100 prompts of this category form the **Initial Business Collection**: ten subcategories of ten prompts each, from `UPL-BIZ-001` to `UPL-BIZ-100`. Each prompt has a permanent ID, a reserved filename and a place in one subcategory. Available prompts link to their English and Serbian files; planned prompts have their ID, title and filename reserved but are not written yet, so they have no links.

Numbering is per category and continues past 100 (`101`, `102`, …) as the collection grows. It does not restart per subcategory.

> **Analysis, not advice.** Prompts in this category support financial, economic and business analysis. They are not financial, tax, accounting, legal or investment advice, and they do not replace an audit, a valuation opinion or professional review. Figures that depend on a date, a jurisdiction or a market (rates, prices, tax rules, multiples) must be verified, dated and sourced.

## Contributing to this category

- **Writing a planned prompt:** open an issue or pull request that references the existing ID (for example `UPL-BIZ-011`). Never assign a different ID to a planned prompt.
- **Proposing a new prompt:** open a prompt suggestion issue first. IDs are assigned through [`catalog.json`](../../../catalog.json) once the proposal is accepted.
- **Improving or translating an available prompt:** keep the ID, filename and front matter consistent in both languages and bump `version` as described in the [prompt format guide](../../../docs/prompt-format.md).

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full process.
