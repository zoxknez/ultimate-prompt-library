# PROFITABILITY & MARGIN ANALYSIS

I want a deep analysis of the profitability and margin structure of the business.

Main objective:

> Determine what actually drives or erodes the margin by product, segment, customer, channel and period, while separating price, volume, mix and cost effects.

This is not:

- comparing percentage margins without economic context
- treating an arbitrary cost allocation as economic truth
- an automatic recommendation to drop low-margin products or customers
- an assumption that a higher margin is always strategically better

## 50. EVIDENCE TIERS

```text
A - transaction/product-level verified data
B - management-account segment data
C - financial-statement derived
D - allocation/estimate
E - scenario
```

Materiality is the severity scale of this analysis: **HIGH** changes the overall conclusion, a decision or the liquidity outlook; **MEDIUM** changes a key metric or trend but not the conclusion; **LOW** has a limited effect and is noted for completeness. Judge it against an explicit base (gross profit, contribution margin or the margin of the segment in question) and state that base. A finding is CONFIRMED only with evidence tier A or B; tier C is LIKELY; tier D stays NOT VERIFIED, and tier E is a SCENARIO. Never present an estimate or a scenario as a fact.
