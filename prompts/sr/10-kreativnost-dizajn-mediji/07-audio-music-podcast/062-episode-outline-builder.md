# IZRADA OUTLINE-A EPIZODE

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite production-grade workflow za "Izrada outline-a epizode", sa planom, tehničkim i kreativnim pravilima, QC kontrolom i jasnim kriterijumima isporuke.

## 1. PRODUKCIONI KONTEKST
Define audience, objective, platform/format, deliverables, technical specs, location/resources, rights/releases, references, schedule, budget and approval owner.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD
Audio production should define listening context, format, sonic identity, intelligibility, pacing and rights. Music and sound design support narrative/brand function rather than filling silence.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS
- format has repeatable structure
- voice is intelligible
- music rights are clear
- sound design has function
- loudness/technical delivery is appropriate
- episode pacing avoids filler
- intro/outro do not overwhelm content
- editing preserves authentic meaning

## 4. PRODUCTION CARD
```text
Asset:
Purpose:
Audience:
Format/spec:
Creative rule:
Technical rule:
Dependency:
Rights/release:
QC criterion:
Delivery version:
```

## 5. OBAVEZNE MATRICE
### Audio Segment Matrix
| Segment | Purpose | Voice/music/SFX | Duration | Transition | QC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Audio Asset Matrix
| Asset | Source/rights | Role | Treatment | Delivery | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid missing rights/release checks, inconsistent treatment, shots/assets without narrative purpose, technical specs discovered too late, edits that break continuity, overproduction without audience value and unclear master/delivery versions.

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT
1. Production brief.
2. Creative/technical rules.
3. Asset/shot/segment plan.
4. Required matrices.
5. QC criteria.
6. Rights/accessibility checks.
7. Delivery specs.
8. Review/revision plan.

End with **Provera produkcionog integriteta** confirming creative intent, technical quality, rights and delivery requirements are all satisfied.
