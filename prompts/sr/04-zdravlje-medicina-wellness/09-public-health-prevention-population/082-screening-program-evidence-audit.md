# AUDIT DOKAZA PROGRAMA SKRININGA

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite transparentnu, populaciono orijentisanu i evidence-based analizu za "Audit dokaza programa skrininga", uz apsolutne rizike, vremenski i geografski kontekst, equity efekte i jasnu procenu neizvesnosti.

## 1. POPULACIONI KONTEKST

Define population, geography, time period, baseline risk, exposure/intervention, comparator, outcome, surveillance system, source date and policy/clinical decision being informed.

## 2. EVIDENCE I SURVEILLANCE

Prioritize current public-health agencies, WHO, national surveillance, high-quality systematic reviews and appropriate epidemiologic studies. Distinguish preliminary surveillance signals from confirmed causal evidence.

## 3. RISK COMMUNICATION

Always show where possible:
- baseline / absolute risk
- relative risk or effect
- timeframe
- denominator
- uncertainty interval
- population to which the estimate applies
- whether the number describes infection, disease, hospitalization, death, screening detection or another outcome

Avoid percentage framing without denominators.

## 4. SPECIJALIZOVANI TEST

For **Audit dokaza programa skrininga**:
- Audit a screening program for population eligibility, net benefit, overdiagnosis, downstream harms, interval and implementation quality.
- test source recency and geographic applicability
- identify selection, measurement and reporting bias
- distinguish individual risk from population effect
- include benefit, harm and implementation burden
- assess differential impact on vulnerable or underserved groups
- state what data would change the conclusion

## 5. MISINFORMATION DEFENSE

Check for:
- cherry-picked time windows
- numerator without denominator
- ecological fallacy
- association presented as causation
- relative risk without absolute risk
- surveillance artifact treated as biological change
- preprint / social post treated as settled evidence
- subgroup claim from underpowered analysis
- false balance between unequal evidence bases
- outdated guidance presented as current

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Population and timeframe.
2. Best current evidence.
3. Absolute and relative effects.
4. Benefit-harm balance.
5. Bias and uncertainty.
6. Equity impact.
7. Misinterpretation risks.
8. Calibrated public-health conclusion.

End with **Provera integriteta javnozdravstvenih dokaza** confirming current source date, denominator, timeframe, population and uncertainty are explicit.
