# PLAN ISTRAŽIVANJA TRŽIŠNE ZARADE

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite praktičan, dokaziv i decision-oriented workflow za "Plan istraživanja tržišne zarade", sa jasnim kriterijumima, trade-offovima i sledećim koracima.

## 1. KONTEKST

Define role, organization, level, responsibilities, stakeholders, constraints, timeline, current evidence and decision/goal.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD

Treat compensation as total package and negotiation as information exchange. Use current market benchmarks when available, but account for geography, level, company type, cash/equity mix, benefits, risk and role scope.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS

- base salary and total compensation are separated
- benchmark sources match geography/level
- equity assumptions are explicit
- benefits and vesting/bonus conditions are included
- walk-away and target positions are defined
- concessions are traded, not given away silently

## 4. DECISION / IMPACT CARD


### Negotiation Ladder

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES

Avoid negotiating against yourself, treating title as proof of impact, confusing visibility with value, delegating without authority/context, feedback without observable examples, or leadership advice with no operating mechanism.

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Context and goal.
2. Evidence base.
3. Options / strategy.
4. Trade-offs and risks.
5. Required matrices.
6. Communication/action plan.
7. Review triggers.
8. Integrity check.

End with **Provera profesionalnog integriteta** confirming that recommendations are tied to actual role context and verified evidence.
