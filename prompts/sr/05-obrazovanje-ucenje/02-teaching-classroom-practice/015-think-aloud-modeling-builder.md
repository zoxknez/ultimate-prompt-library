# IZRADA THINK-ALOUD MODELOVANJA

Glavni cilj:

> Dizajnirajte i auditujte "Izrada think-aloud modelovanja" tako da nastavne odluke reaguju na stvarne dokaze o učenju, a ne na pretpostavke.

## 1. KONTEKST

Capture learner age/stage, subject, prior knowledge, class size, time, language/access needs, target outcomes and likely misconceptions.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- select a task where invisible reasoning matters
- model planning, monitoring and evaluation aloud
- name decision points
- include self-questioning
- avoid narrating irrelevant expert knowledge
- transition learners toward independent use

## 3. EVIDENCE-INFORMED DISCIPLINA

Use strong education evidence as a best-bet starting point, then adapt to subject, learners and implementation context. Separate research evidence, local classroom evidence and professional judgment.

## 4. DOKAZ O UČENJU

```text
Learning objective:
Teacher move:
Learner thinking:
Evidence collected:
Likely error:
If secure:
If insecure:
Follow-up retrieval:
```

## 5. FAILURE MODES

Check for participation mistaken for understanding, leading questions, over-scaffolding, lowered expectations, teacher talk without learner thinking, attractive activity with weak objective alignment and technology without pedagogical purpose.

## 6. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Instructional Decision Matrix
| Objective | Teacher move | Learner thinking | Evidence | Error signal | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Misconception Matrix
| Misconception | Diagnostic evidence | Why plausible | Corrective move | Recheck |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Context and objective.
2. Instructional sequence.
3. Learner-thinking model.
4. Misconceptions.
5. Checks for understanding.
6. Adaptation rules.
7. Retrieval / follow-up plan.
8. Evidence that the strategy worked.

End with **Provera integriteta nastave** confirming that each instructional move has a learning purpose and a concrete way to verify impact.

Ovaj prompt podržava profesionalno planiranje i ne zamenjuje stručni sud nastavnika ili lokalni kurikulum.
