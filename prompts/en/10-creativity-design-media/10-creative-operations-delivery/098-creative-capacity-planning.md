# CREATIVE CAPACITY PLANNING

Main objective:

> Perform a production-grade workflow for Creative Capacity Planning, with clear intent, quality criteria, revision loops and traceable delivery.

## 1. CONTEXT
Define audience, objective, medium, length/format, tone, references, constraints, rights, accessibility, stakeholders, deadline and approval owner.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD
Creative operations should make intake, production, review, versioning, approvals, asset storage and multi-format delivery predictable. The system should reduce coordination cost without suppressing creative iteration.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS
- brief intake is complete
- asset naming/versioning is consistent
- review rounds have purpose and owner
- approvals are traceable
- calendar includes dependencies
- handoff includes source/master/export files
- capacity is visible
- retrospective actions feed the next cycle

## 4. REVIEW CARD
```text
Artifact:
Purpose:
Audience:
Core intent:
Quality criterion:
Evidence/example:
Issue:
Revision:
Owner:
Acceptance:
```

## 5. REQUIRED MATRICES
### Production Pipeline Matrix
| Stage | Input | Owner | Output | Review | SLA/date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Version/Delivery Matrix
| Asset | Master | Version | Format/spec | Destination | Approval | Archive |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid revision by taste alone, narrative elements without function, feedback with no priority, version confusion, unclear approval authority, inaccessible outputs and delivery without preflight.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT
1. Intent and audience.
2. Structure/system.
3. Quality findings.
4. Required matrices.
5. Revision priorities.
6. Acceptance criteria.
7. Delivery/version plan.
8. Final integrity check.

End with **Final Creative Integrity Check** confirming the artifact is coherent, original, accessible, approved and ready for delivery.
