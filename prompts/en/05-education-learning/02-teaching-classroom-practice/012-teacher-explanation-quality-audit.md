# TEACHER EXPLANATION QUALITY AUDIT

Main objective:

> Design and audit Teacher Explanation Quality Audit so instructional decisions respond to real learner evidence rather than assumptions.

## 1. CONTEXT

Capture learner age/stage, subject, prior knowledge, class size, time, language/access needs, target outcomes and likely misconceptions.

## 2. SPECIALIZED WORKFLOW

- identify the exact concept being explained
- remove ambiguous language and unnecessary detail
- use examples and non-examples
- coordinate words and representations
- anticipate likely misconceptions
- check whether learners can explain the idea themselves

## 3. EVIDENCE-INFORMED DISCIPLINE

Use strong education evidence as a best-bet starting point, then adapt to subject, learners and implementation context. Separate research evidence, local classroom evidence and professional judgment.

## 4. EVIDENCE OF LEARNING

```text
Learning objective:
Teacher move:
Learner thinking:
Evidence collected:
Likely error:
If secure:
If insecure:
Follow-up retrieval:
```

## 5. FAILURE MODES

Check for participation mistaken for understanding, leading questions, over-scaffolding, lowered expectations, teacher talk without learner thinking, attractive activity with weak objective alignment and technology without pedagogical purpose.

## 6. REQUIRED MATRICES

### Instructional Decision Matrix
| Objective | Teacher move | Learner thinking | Evidence | Error signal | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Misconception Matrix
| Misconception | Diagnostic evidence | Why plausible | Corrective move | Recheck |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Context and objective.
2. Instructional sequence.
3. Learner-thinking model.
4. Misconceptions.
5. Checks for understanding.
6. Adaptation rules.
7. Retrieval / follow-up plan.
8. Evidence that the strategy worked.

End with **Teaching Integrity Check** confirming that each instructional move has a learning purpose and a concrete way to verify impact.

This supports professional planning and does not replace teacher judgment or local curriculum.
