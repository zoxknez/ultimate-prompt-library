# IZRADA KURSA BACKWARD DESIGN METODOM

Glavni cilj:

> Dizajnirajte ili auditujte "Izrada kursa backward design metodom" tako da kurikulum bude koherentan, sekvenciran prema znanju i usklađen sa ishodima, procenom i realnim vremenom.

## 1. KURIKULARNI KONTEKST

Capture learner stage, subject/domain, duration, mandatory standards, existing outcomes, assessment model, prerequisite assumptions, available time and known attainment gaps.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- start from final outcomes
- define acceptable evidence
- design assessment before activities
- sequence learning experiences backward
- check workload
- verify alignment across all layers

## 3. SEKVENCIRANJE ZNANJA

For every major outcome identify:
```text
Outcome:
Prerequisite knowledge:
New knowledge:
Practice needed:
Likely misconception:
Assessment evidence:
Later dependency:
Retrieval point:
```

## 4. ALIGNMENT GATE

Check:
- outcomes to content
- content to practice
- practice to assessment
- assessment to grading
- prerequisite to sequence
- sequence to available time
- current unit to later transfer

## 5. OVERLOAD TEST

Identify:
- too many outcomes for available time
- content with no downstream value
- assessment consuming disproportionate time
- insufficient guided or independent practice
- missing cumulative review
- abrupt jumps in conceptual complexity
- duplicated content that does not add retrieval or depth

## 6. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Curriculum Sequence Matrix
| Outcome | Prerequisite | Unit | Practice | Assessment | Retrieval | Later dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Alignment Matrix
| Outcome | Taught? | Practiced? | Assessed? | Weighted appropriately? | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Load Matrix
| Unit | New concepts | Time | Practice time | Assessment time | Overload risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Curriculum goals.
2. Prerequisite map.
3. Sequence rationale.
4. Coherence and overload findings.
5. Assessment alignment.
6. Required matrices.
7. Revision priorities.
8. Implementation / transition plan.

End with **Provera integriteta kurikuluma** confirming that each major outcome is taught, practiced, assessed and connected to prerequisite and future learning.

Ovaj prompt podržava dizajn kurikuluma i ne zamenjuje obavezne nacionalne, akreditacione ili institucionalne standarde.
