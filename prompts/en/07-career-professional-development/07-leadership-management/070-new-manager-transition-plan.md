# NEW MANAGER TRANSITION PLAN

Main objective:

> Perform a practical, evidence-based, decision-oriented workflow for New Manager Transition Plan, with explicit criteria, trade-offs and next actions.

## 1. CONTEXT

Define role, organization, level, responsibilities, stakeholders, constraints, timeline, current evidence and decision/goal.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD

Management systems should clarify outcomes, decision rights, context, feedback loops and accountability. Leadership is evaluated through team/system outcomes, not charisma claims.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS

- delegation includes outcome, context, constraints and authority
- one-on-ones are not status meetings only
- coaching preserves employee ownership
- difficult conversations use observable behavior
- team goals connect to organizational outcomes
- decision rights are explicit
- manager feedback includes upward evidence
- team performance diagnosis separates system and individual causes

## 4. DECISION / IMPACT CARD

```text
Objective:

Stakeholder:

Expected impact:
Downside:

```

## 5. REQUIRED MATRICES

### Leadership System Matrix
| Area | Expected behavior/system | Evidence | Gap | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Delegation Matrix
| Outcome | Owner | Authority | Context | Checkpoint | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES

Avoid negotiating against yourself, treating title as proof of impact, confusing visibility with value, delegating without authority/context, feedback without observable examples, or leadership advice with no operating mechanism.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Context and goal.
2. Evidence base.
3. Options / strategy.
4. Trade-offs and risks.
5. Required matrices.
6. Communication/action plan.
7. Review triggers.
8. Integrity check.

End with **Professional Integrity Check** confirming that recommendations are tied to actual role context and verified evidence.

This supports professional development and does not guarantee compensation, promotion or management outcomes.
