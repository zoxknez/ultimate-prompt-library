# IZRADA KREATIVNOG BRIEFA

Glavni cilj:

> Izgradite ili auditujte "Izrada kreativnog briefa" kroz jasan brief, različite kreativne teritorije, ograničenja, reference, kriterijume i iterativni review.

## 1. KREATIVNI KONTEKST
Capture audience, objective, medium, deliverables, brand/context, references, constraints, rights/licensing limits, accessibility needs, timeline, budget and approval owner.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK
- define audience, objective and medium
- capture mandatory constraints
- define desired emotional/functional response
- collect reference directions without copying
- set success criteria
- identify approvals and deliverables

## 3. REFERENCE DISCIPLINA
Use references to extract principles, not to reproduce protected assets or imitate a living creator's distinctive style. Separate inspiration, category convention and ownable direction.

## 4. CONCEPT CARD
```text
Concept:
Audience response:
Core idea:
Visual/narrative rule:
Reference principle:
Constraint:
Differentiator:
Feasibility:
Risk:
Acceptance criterion:
```

## 5. OBAVEZNE MATRICE
### Concept Matrix
| Concept | Strategic fit | Distinctiveness | Feasibility | Extensibility | Risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Reference Matrix
| Reference | Useful principle | What not to copy | Application | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Acceptance Matrix
| Criterion | Must/Should | Evidence | Reviewer | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid first-idea fixation, cosmetic variants, reference copying, moodboards without rules, novelty without purpose, approval criteria defined too late and subjective feedback with no link to objective.

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT
1. Brief.
2. Creative territories.
3. Reference principles.
4. Constraints.
5. Required matrices.
6. Selected direction.
7. Risks.
8. Iteration/approval plan.

End with **Provera kreativnog integriteta** confirming the selected concept is distinct, feasible, relevant and not dependent on copying protected expression.
