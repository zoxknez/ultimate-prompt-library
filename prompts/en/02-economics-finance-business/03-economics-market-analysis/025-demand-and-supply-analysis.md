# DEMAND & SUPPLY ANALYSIS

I want a deep analysis of the demand and supply dynamics of a specific market, product, service or input.

Main objective:

> Determine what moves demand and supply, where imbalances arise, how they pass through to price, volume, margins and capacity, and whether the observed movement is a lasting structural trend or a temporary shock.

This is not:

- drawing a theoretical supply-demand curve without data
- an assumption that every price move comes from demand
- automatically declaring a shortage when the price rises

## 58. FALSE POSITIVE RULES

Price up does not prove demand up.

Inventory down does not prove shortage.

High utilization does not automatically mean supply constrained.

## 59. EVIDENCE TIERS

```text
A - direct market volume/capacity/inventory data
B - multiple consistent operating indicators
C - strong derived market balance
D - inferred imbalance
E - scenario
```

Materiality is the severity scale of this analysis: **HIGH** changes the overall conclusion or the decision; **MEDIUM** changes a key metric, driver or trend but not the conclusion; **LOW** has a limited effect and is noted for completeness. Judge it against an explicit base (market volume, price, capacity or the margin of the business in question) and state that base. A finding is CONFIRMED only with evidence tier A or B; tier C is LIKELY; tier D stays NOT VERIFIED, and tier E is a SCENARIO. Never present an estimate, a forecast or a scenario as a fact.
