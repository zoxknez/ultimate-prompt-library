# CAREER DIRECTION AUDIT

Main objective:

> Perform an evidence-based, market-aware workflow for Career Direction Audit without inventing experience or relying on generic motivational advice.

## 1. CAREER CONTEXT

Capture current role/level, years and type of experience, verified achievements, preferred work, constraints, location/remote needs, compensation floor, target industries, timeline and risk tolerance.

## 2. SPECIALIZED WORKFLOW

- inventory roles, tasks and environments that consistently produce strong performance
- separate evidence from preference
- identify constraints and non-negotiables
- compare adjacent career paths
- define 6-12 month experiments
- set review criteria

## 3. EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

Every claimed strength, fit or achievement should map to:
- a concrete project, task or decision
- observed behavior
- result / metric / outcome where available
- stakeholder or artifact evidence
- recency and relevance

Never invent metrics, titles, scope or responsibilities.

## 4. MARKET REALITY GATE

Use real target-role data where available. Distinguish:
- candidate-controlled factors
- employer-controlled factors
- market conditions
- timing/luck
- credential signals
- actual capability

Do not promise interview or hiring outcomes.

## 5. DECISION CARD

```text
Option:

Downside:
```

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Decision Risk Matrix
| Choice | Upside | Downside | Uncertainty | Reversible? | Next evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Current evidence base.
2. Target role/options.
3. Strength and gap map.
4. Positioning.
5. Market/reality constraints.
6. Experiments and next steps.
7. Risk controls.
8. Review date and criteria.

End with **Career Strategy Integrity Check** confirming that recommendations are tied to verified experience and explicit assumptions.

This supports career planning and does not guarantee hiring, promotion or compensation outcomes.
