# VOICE OF CUSTOMER RESEARCH PLAN

Main objective:

> Perform Voice of Customer Research Plan using real customer evidence, behavior, cohorts, retention mechanisms and measurable growth outcomes.

## 1. CUSTOMER CONTEXT
Define segment, customer stage, product/use case, activation event, value received, churn/retention window, research sources and growth objective.

## 2. CUSTOMER STANDARD
- separate stated preference from observed behavior
- use interviews, support, sales and product data together
- distinguish acquisition problem from activation/retention problem
- analyze churn by reason and cohort
- identify expansion only after value realization
- treat referral as a product/value signal, not only an incentive mechanic
- test growth loops for closed-loop behavior and saturation

## 3. CUSTOMER CARD
```text
Segment:
Job/problem:
Trigger:
Activation:
Value moment:
Retention driver:
Churn risk:
Expansion signal:
Referral signal:
Evidence:
```

## 4. REQUIRED MATRICES
### Retention Matrix
| Segment/cohort | Activation | Retention | Churn reason | Driver | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### VOC Matrix
| Theme | Evidence source | Frequency | Severity/value | Contradiction | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. FAILURE MODES
Avoid confusing NPS or interview sentiment with behavior, treating all churn equally, pushing expansion before value, incentive-only referrals and growth loops that depend on one-time arbitrage.

## 6. REQUIRED OUTPUT
1. Customer/segment evidence.
2. Activation/retention diagnosis.
3. VOC/churn findings.
4. Growth/expansion opportunities.
5. Required matrices.
6. Experiments.
7. Measurement.
8. Review triggers.

End with **Customer Growth Integrity Check** confirming growth recommendations are tied to observed customer value and retention evidence.

This prompt does not guarantee retention, expansion or referral growth.
