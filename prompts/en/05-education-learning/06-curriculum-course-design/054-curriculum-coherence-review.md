# CURRICULUM COHERENCE REVIEW

Main objective:

> Design or audit Curriculum Coherence Review so curriculum is coherent, knowledge-sequenced and aligned with outcomes, assessment and realistic instructional time.

## 1. CURRICULUM CONTEXT

Capture learner stage, subject/domain, duration, mandatory standards, existing outcomes, assessment model, prerequisite assumptions, available time and known attainment gaps.

## 2. SPECIALIZED WORKFLOW

- test vertical progression across years/levels
- test horizontal coherence within a stage
- remove duplication without loss of retrieval
- identify missing bridges
- align terminology
- verify assessment coverage

## 3. KNOWLEDGE SEQUENCING

For every major outcome identify:
```text
Outcome:
Prerequisite knowledge:
New knowledge:
Practice needed:
Likely misconception:
Assessment evidence:
Later dependency:
Retrieval point:
```

## 4. ALIGNMENT GATE

Check:
- outcomes to content
- content to practice
- practice to assessment
- assessment to grading
- prerequisite to sequence
- sequence to available time
- current unit to later transfer

## 5. OVERLOAD TEST

Identify:
- too many outcomes for available time
- content with no downstream value
- assessment consuming disproportionate time
- insufficient guided or independent practice
- missing cumulative review
- abrupt jumps in conceptual complexity
- duplicated content that does not add retrieval or depth

## 6. REQUIRED MATRICES

### Curriculum Sequence Matrix
| Outcome | Prerequisite | Unit | Practice | Assessment | Retrieval | Later dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Alignment Matrix
| Outcome | Taught? | Practiced? | Assessed? | Weighted appropriately? | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Load Matrix
| Unit | New concepts | Time | Practice time | Assessment time | Overload risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Curriculum goals.
2. Prerequisite map.
3. Sequence rationale.
4. Coherence and overload findings.
5. Assessment alignment.
6. Required matrices.
7. Revision priorities.
8. Implementation / transition plan.

End with **Curriculum Integrity Check** confirming that each major outcome is taught, practiced, assessed and connected to prerequisite and future learning.

This supports curriculum design and does not replace mandatory national, accreditation or institutional standards.
