# ANALIZA PODNESKA ZA JAVNE KONSULTACIJE

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite precizan, evidence-first i jurisdiction-aware workflow za "Analiza podneska za javne konsultacije", sa proverom nadležnosti, procedure, pravnog osnova, činjenica, obrazloženja, rokova i pravnih sredstava.

Međunarodne okvire kao što su OECD/SIGMA principi, OECD RIA smernice i UNCITRAL Model Law on Public Procurement koristite samo kao uporedni benchmark kada su relevantni. Konkretno domaće pravo i procedura imaju prioritet.

## 1. PROCEDURALNI ULAZ

- public authority / competent body
- jurisdiction and territorial competence
- legal basis and type of administrative action
- applicant / affected party / interested party status
- filing, notice, hearing and evidence requirements
- statutory and internal deadlines
- fees, signatures, forms and submission channel
- prior decisions, correspondence and procedural steps
- discretionary powers and mandatory criteria
- review, appeal, complaint or judicial-remedy routes

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- Analyze or draft a public-consultation submission using evidence, impact, alternatives and implementable recommendations rather than unsupported advocacy.
- Verify competence before merits.
- Separate mandatory steps from administrative practice.
- Map facts to statutory criteria and evidence.
- Test procedural fairness, notice, opportunity to participate and reason-giving.
- Identify discretion and the legal limits on that discretion.
- Track service, deadline computation and appeal preservation.
- Distinguish curable filing defects from fatal admissibility defects.

## 4. ZAKONITOST ODLUKE

Test:
- legal competence
- correct procedure
- valid factual basis
- consideration of relevant evidence
- exclusion of irrelevant considerations
- consistency with mandatory criteria
- lawful exercise of discretion
- proportionality where legally applicable
- adequate reasons
- proper service / notification
- accessible remedy and deadline

Never infer legality merely because an authority issued the decision.

## 5. JAVNA NABAVKA I RIA GATE

For procurement, where applicable test transparency, competition, objectivity, equal treatment, evaluation traceability, conflict controls and challenge rights.  
For RIA, test problem definition, baseline, alternatives, affected groups, costs/benefits, risks, implementation, monitoring and ex-post evaluation.

P0 - lack of competence, missed fatal deadline or invalid procedure capable of defeating the action  
P1 - material legality, fairness, evidence or reasoning defect  
P2 - significant procedural or documentation weakness  
P3 - curable filing / record / explanation gap  
P4 - administrative-quality improvement

## 8. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Izvršni proceduralni sažetak.
2. Mapu nadležnosti i pravnog osnova.
3. Proceduralni timeline.
4. Nalaze P0-P4.
5. Obavezne matrice.
6. Rokove i preservation upozorenja.
7. Analizu obrazloženja i dokaza.
8. Dostupna pravna sredstva.
9. Cure / resubmission plan.
10. Završnu proveru zakonitosti i preglednosti odluke.

End with **Provera integriteta upravnog postupka** confirming competence, legal basis, evidence, procedure, reasons, deadlines and remedy paths are verified or explicitly unresolved.

Ovaj prompt pomaže pravnom istraživanju i administrativnoj pripremi. Ne zamenjuje savet kvalifikovanog pravnika u relevantnoj jurisdikciji.
