# PLAN ISTRAŽIVANJA TRŽIŠTA

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite evidence-based i commercially grounded workflow za "Plan istraživanja tržišta", sa jasnim segmentom, problemom, ponudom, kanalom i merljivim poslovnim ishodom.

## 1. POSLOVNI KONTEKST

Capture product/service, business model, market, customer type, price/ACV, buying cycle, current funnel, constraints, competitors, existing evidence and primary growth objective.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- define decisions research must support
- choose qualitative and quantitative methods appropriately
- identify sample and recruitment bias
- write neutral questions
- predefine synthesis method
- connect findings to action

## 3. EVIDENCE DISCIPLINA

Separate:
- customer evidence
- market evidence
- competitor evidence
- internal analytics
- sales anecdotes
- hypothesis
- assumption

Do not convert a loud anecdote into a market fact.

## 4. STRATEGY CARD

```text
Segment:
Problem:
Trigger:
Alternative:
Offer:
Differentiator:
Proof:
Channel:
Conversion event:
Revenue/value metric:
Main assumption:
Experiment:
```

## 5. METRIC HIERARCHY

Distinguish:
- attention metrics
- engagement metrics
- lead/conversion metrics
- activation/revenue metrics
- retention/LTV metrics
- efficiency metrics

Do not treat impressions, clicks or followers as business outcomes unless the objective is explicitly reach/awareness.

## 6. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Segment-Problem Matrix
| Segment | Problem | Trigger | Alternative | Evidence | Value | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Channel Fit Matrix
| Channel | Audience fit | Intent | Cost | Learning speed | Conversion path | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Assumption-Test Matrix
| Assumption | Evidence | Risk if wrong | Cheapest test | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Business objective.
2. Market/segment evidence.
3. Positioning/strategy.
4. Channel priorities.
5. Assumptions and risks.
6. Required matrices.
7. Experiments.
8. Measurement plan.

End with **Provera integriteta marketinške strategije** confirming that strategy choices are tied to customer/market evidence and measurable outcomes.

Ovaj prompt podržava marketinško planiranje i ne garantuje rast, prihod ili performanse kampanje.
