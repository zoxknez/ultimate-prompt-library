# PROMOTION READINESS AUDIT

Main objective:

> Perform a practical, evidence-based, decision-oriented workflow for Promotion Readiness Audit, with explicit criteria, trade-offs and next actions.

## 1. CONTEXT

Define role, organization, level, responsibilities, stakeholders, constraints, timeline, current evidence and decision/goal.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD

Workplace growth should be based on role expectations, measurable outcomes, stakeholder evidence and increasing scope. Separate impact from self-promotion, and promotion readiness from entitlement.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS

- expectations are clarified early
- goals are measurable and role-relevant
- evidence of impact is maintained continuously
- feedback is specific and actionable
- promotion gaps are mapped to actual level criteria
- visibility follows value creation
- scope expansion has authority and capacity

## 4. DECISION / IMPACT CARD

```text
Objective:
```

## 5. REQUIRED MATRICES

### Performance Evidence Matrix
| Goal | Action | Outcome | Metric | Stakeholder evidence | Level signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Growth Gap Matrix
| Capability | Current evidence | Next-level expectation | Gap | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES

Avoid negotiating against yourself, treating title as proof of impact, confusing visibility with value, delegating without authority/context, feedback without observable examples, or leadership advice with no operating mechanism.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Context and goal.
2. Evidence base.
3. Options / strategy.
4. Trade-offs and risks.
5. Required matrices.
6. Communication/action plan.
7. Review triggers.
8. Integrity check.

End with **Professional Integrity Check** confirming that recommendations are tied to actual role context and verified evidence.

This supports professional development and does not guarantee compensation, promotion or management outcomes.
