# AUDIT KOMPOZICIJE I KADRA

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite production-grade workflow za "Audit kompozicije i kadra", sa planom, tehničkim i kreativnim pravilima, QC kontrolom i jasnim kriterijumima isporuke.

## 1. PRODUKCIONI KONTEKST
Define audience, objective, platform/format, deliverables, technical specs, location/resources, rights/releases, references, schedule, budget and approval owner.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD
Photography planning should connect every shot to a communication purpose. Define composition, light, lens/perspective, subject direction, background, crop variants and edit treatment before production where possible.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS
- shot has purpose
- lighting supports mood/product truth
- perspective avoids misleading distortion
- crop variants match channels
- release/licensing is cleared
- editing preserves required truthfulness
- series has visual continuity
- library selection avoids generic mismatch

## 4. PRODUCTION CARD
```text
Asset:
Purpose:
Audience:
Format/spec:
Creative rule:
Technical rule:
Dependency:
Rights/release:
QC criterion:
Delivery version:
```

## 5. OBAVEZNE MATRICE
### Shot Matrix
| Shot | Purpose | Composition | Light | Lens/perspective | Variants | QC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Image Selection Matrix
| Asset | Message fit | Technical quality | Rights | Diversity/context fit | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid missing rights/release checks, inconsistent treatment, shots/assets without narrative purpose, technical specs discovered too late, edits that break continuity, overproduction without audience value and unclear master/delivery versions.

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT
1. Production brief.
2. Creative/technical rules.
3. Asset/shot/segment plan.
4. Required matrices.
5. QC criteria.
6. Rights/accessibility checks.
7. Delivery specs.
8. Review/revision plan.

End with **Provera produkcionog integriteta** confirming creative intent, technical quality, rights and delivery requirements are all satisfied.
