# AUDIT STRUKTURE AKADEMSKOG PISANJA

Glavni cilj:

> Izgradite evidence-informed "Audit strukture akademskog pisanja" koji razvija razumevanje, izražavanje i predmetno-specifičnu pismenost kroz eksplicitnu nastavu, praksu i proveru transfera.

## 1. JEZIČKI I LITERACY KONTEKST

Capture learner age/stage, language background, current proficiency, text/task demands, subject, known reading/writing needs, accessibility needs and target outcome.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- audit thesis, argument, evidence and organization
- separate content from style
- check source integration
- identify unsupported claims
- improve paragraph logic
- preserve author voice

## 3. KOMPONENTNI MODEL

Separate where relevant:
- decoding / word recognition
- fluency
- vocabulary
- syntax / language comprehension
- background knowledge
- inference
- text structure
- writing composition
- disciplinary conventions
- oral language

Do not treat "reading difficulty" or "weak writing" as a single undifferentiated problem.

## 4. MODEL PRAKSE

```text
Target:
Model:
Guided practice:
Independent practice:
Feedback:
Retrieval / reuse:
Transfer task:
Evidence:
```

## 5. FAILURE MODES

Check for:
- strategy taught without meaningful text/content
- vocabulary taught once and never reused
- writing feedback too broad to act on
- language errors interpreted as lack of subject knowledge
- support that hides whether learner can perform independently
- text complexity mismatched to instructional purpose
- assessment requiring language not explicitly taught

## 6. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Literacy Demand Matrix
| Task/text | Vocabulary | Knowledge | Structure | Reasoning | Language demand | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Skill Evidence Matrix
| Domain | Baseline | Current evidence | Support level | Transfer | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Feedback Matrix
| Priority | Evidence | Feedback | Learner action | Recheck |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Literacy/language profile.
2. Target skill.
3. Explicit teaching sequence.
4. Practice and feedback.
5. Required matrices.
6. Transfer task.
7. Progress evidence.
8. Adaptation / specialist referral trigger where appropriate.

End with **Provera integriteta pismenosti** confirming that the identified difficulty matches the evidence and that support targets the relevant component rather than a vague label.

Ovaj prompt podržava obrazovni rad i ne zamenjuje specijalističku procenu logopeda, psihologa ili drugog stručnjaka kada je potrebna.
