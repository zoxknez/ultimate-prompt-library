# EXERCISE READINESS & SAFETY SCREEN

Main objective:

> Screen readiness for exercise, contraindications, symptoms and need for clinical clearance before planning training.

## 1. SAFETY SCREEN

Before planning exercise identify chest pain/pressure, unexplained fainting, severe breathlessness, acute neurologic symptoms, uncontrolled major illness, severe injury, significant infection, pregnancy-specific warning signs or clinician-imposed restrictions. These require appropriate professional assessment before routine exercise advice.

## 2. CONTEXT

Capture baseline activity, age, relevant diagnoses, symptoms, injuries, medicines affecting exercise response, functional limits, available equipment, environment, goals, preferences and time.

## 3. FITT-VP & PROGRESSION

Plan with:
- frequency
- intensity using appropriate methods
- time / duration
- type
- total volume
- progression
- recovery
- stop / modify criteria

Use talk test, RPE or heart-rate methods only when appropriate to the person and medication context.

## 4. WHO / GUIDELINE CONTEXT

Use current physical-activity guidance for the relevant age and population, but adapt recommendations for pregnancy, disability, chronic conditions, rehabilitation stage and functional capacity. Do not copy population targets as an individualized prescription without context.

## 5. LOAD & RECOVERY

Track:
```text
Session:
Mode:
Duration:
Intensity:
Load:
Symptoms during:
Symptoms after:
Recovery:
Sleep:
Pain / function:
Next-session adjustment:
```

Avoid treating any single wearable score, soreness value or heart-rate metric as definitive.

## 6. REHAB / RETURN GATE

For injury or rehabilitation, progression should depend on tissue/condition constraints, clinician instructions, pain/symptom behavior, function and load tolerance. Do not advise "push through" neurologic, cardiopulmonary or severe pain warning signs.

## 8. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Safety screen.
2. Baseline and goals.
3. Exercise / rehabilitation plan.
4. Progression criteria.
5. Recovery plan.
6. Stop / modify / urgent escalation rules.
7. Tracking metrics.
8. Conditions requiring clinician or physiotherapist input.

End with **Exercise Safety Check** confirming the plan is progressive, individualized, symptom-aware and does not override clinical restrictions.

This does not replace medical evaluation or an individualized rehabilitation plan from a qualified professional.
