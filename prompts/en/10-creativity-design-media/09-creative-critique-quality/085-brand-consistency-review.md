# BRAND CONSISTENCY REVIEW

Main objective:

> Perform a production-grade workflow for Brand Consistency Review, with clear intent, quality criteria, revision loops and traceable delivery.

## 1. CONTEXT
Define audience, objective, medium, length/format, tone, references, constraints, rights, accessibility, stakeholders, deadline and approval owner.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD
Creative critique should evaluate work against brief, audience, system rules and acceptance criteria before personal taste. Feedback should be prioritized, actionable and distinguish defect, risk and preference.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS
- brief criteria are visible
- feedback cites observable issue
- severity/priority is explicit
- accessibility is included
- brand/system consistency is checked
- originality concerns are evidence-based
- cross-format variants are reviewed
- preflight covers technical delivery

## 4. REVIEW CARD

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid revision by taste alone, narrative elements without function, feedback with no priority, version confusion, unclear approval authority, inaccessible outputs and delivery without preflight.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT
1. Intent and audience.
2. Structure/system.
3. Quality findings.
4. Required matrices.
5. Revision priorities.
6. Acceptance criteria.
7. Delivery/version plan.
8. Final integrity check.

End with **Final Creative Integrity Check** confirming the artifact is coherent, original, accessible, approved and ready for delivery.
