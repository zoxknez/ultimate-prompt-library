# REASONABLE ACCOMMODATION PLANNER

Main objective:

> Design Reasonable Accommodation Planner to remove barriers to learning and participation while preserving high expectations, assessment validity and increasing learner independence.

## 1. LEARNER & BARRIER CONTEXT

Capture strengths, learning goals, observed barriers, current supports, communication/language needs, sensory/physical access, assistive technology, assessment conditions and specialist recommendations where available.

## 2. SPECIALIZED WORKFLOW

- define the barrier and required access outcome
- choose least intrusive effective accommodation
- separate accommodation from curriculum modification
- document implementation
- check unintended disadvantage
- review periodically

## 3. ACCESS WITHOUT LOWERING THE CONSTRUCT

For each adaptation state:
```text
Barrier:
Learning objective / construct:
Support / accommodation:
Why it helps:
What must remain unchanged:
Independence goal:
Evidence of benefit:
Review date:
```

## 4. UNIVERSAL FIRST, INDIVIDUAL WHEN NEEDED

First remove avoidable barriers for everyone where feasible. Add individualized support when universal design is insufficient. Do not use diagnosis labels as complete descriptions of learning needs.

## 5. FAIRNESS & DIGNITY

Check:
- access to the same meaningful learning
- stigma / segregation risk
- learner voice
- privacy
- consistency of accommodations
- effect on independence
- whether support accidentally reveals answers or changes the construct

## 6. REQUIRED MATRICES

### Barrier-Support Matrix
| Barrier | Learning impact | Universal change | Individual support | Evidence | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Participation Matrix
| Activity | Access | Role | Support | Independent? | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Accommodation Validity Matrix
| Accommodation | Construct changed? | Benefit | Risk | Allowed / required source | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Learner strengths and goals.
2. Barrier analysis.
3. Universal design actions.
4. Individual supports/accommodations.
5. Participation plan.
6. Assessment-validity check.
7. Independence/fading plan.
8. Progress review criteria.

End with **Inclusion Integrity Check** confirming that supports increase genuine access and participation without unnecessarily lowering learning goals or creating dependency.

This supports inclusive educational planning and does not replace formal specialist assessment, individualized education plans or legal obligations.
