# AUDIT KONVERZIJE SALES PIPELINE-A

Glavni cilj:

> Dizajnirajte ili auditujte "Audit konverzije sales pipeline-a" tako da sales proces otkriva stvarni problem, kvalifikuje fit i vodi ka odluci bez manipulativnog pritiska.

## 1. SALES CONTEXT
Define buyer, problem, urgency, current solution, stakeholders, decision process, budget context, evidence, objections and stage.

## 2. SALES STANDARD
- discovery before pitching
- qualify problem, authority, urgency and fit
- separate curiosity from buying intent
- tie demo to discovered use case
- handle objections by clarifying, not overpowering
- proposals reflect agreed problem/scope/value
- follow-up has a reason and next decision
- disqualify poor-fit opportunities

## 3. DEAL CARD
```text
Problem:
Impact:
Current solution:
Trigger:
Decision criteria:
Stakeholders:
Evidence:
Objection:
Next step:
Risk:
```

## 4. OBAVEZNE MATRICE
### Qualification Matrix
| Criterion | Evidence | Confidence | Gap | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Objection Matrix
| Objection | Underlying concern | Evidence needed | Response | Next question |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. FAILURE MODES
Avoid pitching before discovery, fake urgency, hiding poor fit, demo feature dumping, chasing unqualified deals and follow-ups with no decision relevance.

## 6. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT
1. Buyer/problem context.
2. Qualification.
3. Discovery/demo/message.
4. Objections.
5. Required matrices.
6. Next-step plan.
7. Disqualification criteria.
8. Conversion risks.

End with **Sales integritet provera** confirming the process is evidence-based, fit-aware and non-deceptive.

Ovaj prompt ne garantuje zatvaranje prodaje ili prihod.
