# IZRADA STRUKTURE CASE INTERVJUA

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite praktičan, evidence-based workflow za "Izrada strukture case intervjua", sa verifikovanim iskustvom, jasnim signalima i bez izmišljanja podataka.

## 1. KONTEKST

Define target role/company, actual experience, achievements, constraints, seniority, location/remote preference, application stage and desired outcome.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD

Interview preparation should improve retrieval and structure, not create fictional scripts. Behavioral answers must come from real situations. Technical/case work should show assumptions, reasoning, trade-offs and verification.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS

- stories are real and reusable across competencies
- STAR structure preserves the actual situation
- technical claims are checked
- case assumptions are explicit
- take-home scope and time are controlled
- interview questions gather decision-relevant information
- debrief distinguishes facts from emotional impression

## 4. EVIDENCE CARD

```text
Claim:
Source experience:
Action:
Scope:
Result:
Metric:
Context:
Relevance:
Confidence:
```

## 5. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Story Bank Matrix
| Competency | Situation | Action | Result | Evidence | Risk/question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Interview Gap Matrix
| Requirement | Evidence/story | Confidence | Weakness | Prep action |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES

Avoid fabricated metrics, generic adjectives, keyword stuffing, vague outreach, rehearsed answers with no evidence, irrelevant portfolio material and activity metrics that do not improve conversion.

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Target and context.
2. Evidence inventory.
3. Fit/gap analysis.
4. Draft or workflow.
5. Quality risks.
6. Required matrices.
7. Next action.
8. Verification checklist.

End with **Provera integriteta prijave** confirming that every material claim is supported by real experience or explicitly marked as an assumption.

Ovaj prompt podržava karijerni rad i ne garantuje selekciju ili ponudu.
