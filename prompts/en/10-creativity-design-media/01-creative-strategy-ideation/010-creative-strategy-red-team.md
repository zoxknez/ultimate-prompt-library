# CREATIVE STRATEGY RED TEAM

Main objective:

> Build or audit Creative Strategy Red Team through a clear brief, distinct creative territories, constraints, references, criteria and iterative review.

## 1. CREATIVE CONTEXT
Capture audience, objective, medium, deliverables, brand/context, references, constraints, rights/licensing limits, accessibility needs, timeline, budget and approval owner.

## 2. SPECIALIZED WORKFLOW
- attack genericness, inconsistency, feasibility, audience fit, reference dependence and acceptance criteria
- identify strongest failure scenario
- test whether concept survives format changes
- surface hidden assumptions
- recommend redesign
- state what to keep

## 3. REFERENCE DISCIPLINE
Use references to extract principles, not to reproduce protected assets or imitate a living creator's distinctive style. Separate inspiration, category convention and ownable direction.

## 4. CONCEPT CARD
```text
Concept:

Visual/narrative rule:

```

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Reference Matrix

|---|---|---|---|---|

### Acceptance Matrix
| Criterion | Must/Should | Evidence | Reviewer | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid first-idea fixation, cosmetic variants, reference copying, moodboards without rules, novelty without purpose, approval criteria defined too late and subjective feedback with no link to objective.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT
1. Brief.
2. Creative territories.
3. Reference principles.
4. Constraints.
5. Required matrices.
6. Selected direction.
7. Risks.
8. Iteration/approval plan.

End with **Creative Integrity Check** confirming the selected concept is distinct, feasible, relevant and not dependent on copying protected expression.
