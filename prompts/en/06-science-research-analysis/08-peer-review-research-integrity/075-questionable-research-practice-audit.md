# QUESTIONABLE RESEARCH PRACTICE AUDIT

Main objective:

> Perform a rigorous, transparent workflow for Questionable Research Practice Audit that preserves research integrity, decision traceability and claims proportional to evidence.

## 1. CONTEXT

Define research object, study/model/artifact, responsible people, data/code/materials, decision stage, relevant standards, risks and intended use.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD

Use reporting and integrity frameworks for their intended purpose. STROBE explicitly notes that its checklist improves reporting and is not an instrument for evaluating study quality. Apply authorship, conflict, ethics, correction and transparency rules using the relevant journal, institution, funder and field standards.

## 3. SPECIALIZED FOCUS

For **Questionable Research Practice Audit**:
- define the exact integrity/model/project question
- identify source documents and evidence
- separate mandatory requirements from good practice
- identify hidden assumptions and incentives
- test failure, misuse and edge cases
- produce corrective actions with verification

## 4. INTEGRITY CHECKS

- authorship reflects actual contributions
- conflicts and funding are disclosed
- ethics approvals/consent are traceable where required
- reporting guideline matches study design
- primary outcomes and analyses are not selectively omitted
- image/data anomalies are investigated neutrally
- corrections/retractions are proportionate to evidence
- data/code/material transparency matches consent and governance

## 7. ANTI-OVERCLAIM / ANTI-BLAME RULES

Do not infer misconduct from anomaly alone, treat reporting compliance as proof of validity, select a model only by fit, hide failed replications, use stakeholder preference as evidence, or convert uncertain evidence into deterministic policy claims.

## 8. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Scope and evidence base.
2. Standard/method applied.
3. Key findings.
4. Required matrices.
5. Alternative explanations / models.
6. Robustness or verification plan.
7. Corrective / implementation actions.
8. Residual limitations.

End with **Final Integrity Check** confirming traceability from evidence to recommendation or scientific claim.

This supports scientific work and does not replace required formal ethical, institutional, regulatory or specialist procedures.
