# AUDIT PRUNING-A CONTENT PORTFOLIJA

Glavni cilj:

> Izgradite evidence-based "Audit pruning-a content portfolija" koji povezuje stvarnu potrebu publike, jedinstven ugao, distribuciju i merljiv poslovni rezultat.

## 1. KONTEKST
Define audience, topic/problem, funnel role, format, channel, proof, business objective, distribution resources and success metric.

## 2. CONTENT STANDARD
- each asset maps to a real audience need
- content has a distinct angle or useful synthesis
- evidence and examples support claims
- distribution is planned before publication
- repurposing preserves context
- thought leadership reflects real expertise
- publishing cadence does not outrank quality
- metrics match the role of the asset

## 3. CONTENT CARD
```text
Audience:
Question/problem:
Angle:
Evidence:
Format:
Distribution:
CTA:
Funnel role:
Success metric:
Refresh/prune trigger:
```

## 4. OBAVEZNE MATRICE

### Content Portfolio Matrix
| Asset/topic | Audience need | Funnel role | Differentiation | Distribution | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Editorial Priority Matrix
| Idea | Evidence/demand | Business fit | Effort | Reuse potential | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. FAILURE MODES
Avoid content with no distribution, generic summaries with no added value, thought leadership beyond real expertise, vanity-metric optimization and pruning based only on age.

## 6. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT
1. Audience/problem.
2. Content angle.
3. Evidence/proof.
4. Brief or editorial structure.
5. Distribution plan.
6. Required matrices.
7. Measurement.
8. Refresh/prune logic.

End with **Provera integriteta sadržaja** confirming the content has a user purpose, evidence and business role.

Ovaj prompt ne garantuje reach, engagement ili prihod.
