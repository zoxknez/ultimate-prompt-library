# MATRICA ODLUKE IZMEĐU KARIJERNIH OPCIJA

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite evidence-based i market-aware workflow za "Matrica odluke između karijernih opcija", bez izmišljanja iskustva i bez generičkog motivacionog sadržaja.

## 1. KARIJERNI KONTEKST

Capture current role/level, years and type of experience, verified achievements, preferred work, constraints, location/remote needs, compensation floor, target industries, timeline and risk tolerance.

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- define decision criteria and weights
- score options with evidence
- separate uncertainty from preference
- run sensitivity on weights
- identify irreversible choices
- state information worth gathering before decision

## 3. EVIDENCE DISCIPLINA

Every claimed strength, fit or achievement should map to:
- a concrete project, task or decision
- observed behavior
- result / metric / outcome where available
- stakeholder or artifact evidence
- recency and relevance

Never invent metrics, titles, scope or responsibilities.

## 4. MARKET REALITY GATE

Use real target-role data where available. Distinguish:
- candidate-controlled factors
- employer-controlled factors
- market conditions
- timing/luck
- credential signals
- actual capability

Do not promise interview or hiring outcomes.

## 5. DECISION CARD

```text
Option:

Skill gap:
Signal gap:
Market demand:
Cost/time:
Downside:
Upside:
Reversibility:
```

## 7. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Current evidence base.
2. Target role/options.
3. Strength and gap map.
4. Positioning.
5. Market/reality constraints.
6. Experiments and next steps.
7. Risk controls.
8. Review date and criteria.

End with **Provera integriteta karijerne strategije** confirming that recommendations are tied to verified experience and explicit assumptions.

Ovaj prompt podržava karijerno planiranje i ne garantuje zapošljavanje, promociju ili nivo kompenzacije.
