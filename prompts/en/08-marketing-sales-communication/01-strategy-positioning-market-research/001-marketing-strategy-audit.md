# MARKETING STRATEGY AUDIT

Main objective:

> Perform an evidence-based, commercially grounded workflow for Marketing Strategy Audit, with a clear segment, problem, offer, channel and measurable business outcome.

## 1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Capture product/service, business model, market, customer type, price/ACV, buying cycle, current funnel, constraints, competitors, existing evidence and primary growth objective.

## 2. SPECIALIZED WORKFLOW

- define business objective and market constraint
- separate strategy from campaign tactics
- identify target segments and priority problems
- map offer and channel fit
- define measurement hierarchy
- state what will not be pursued

## 3. EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

Separate:
- customer evidence
- market evidence
- competitor evidence
- internal analytics
- sales anecdotes
- hypothesis
- assumption

Do not convert a loud anecdote into a market fact.

## 4. STRATEGY CARD

```text
Segment:
Problem:
Trigger:
Alternative:
Offer:
Differentiator:
Proof:
Channel:
Conversion event:
Revenue/value metric:
Main assumption:
Experiment:
```

## 5. METRIC HIERARCHY

Distinguish:
- attention metrics
- engagement metrics
- lead/conversion metrics
- activation/revenue metrics
- retention/LTV metrics
- efficiency metrics

Do not treat impressions, clicks or followers as business outcomes unless the objective is explicitly reach/awareness.

## 6. REQUIRED MATRICES

### Segment-Problem Matrix
| Segment | Problem | Trigger | Alternative | Evidence | Value | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Channel Fit Matrix
| Channel | Audience fit | Intent | Cost | Learning speed | Conversion path | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Assumption-Test Matrix
| Assumption | Evidence | Risk if wrong | Cheapest test | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT

1. Business objective.
2. Market/segment evidence.
3. Positioning/strategy.
4. Channel priorities.
5. Assumptions and risks.
6. Required matrices.
7. Experiments.
8. Measurement plan.

End with **Marketing Strategy Integrity Check** confirming that strategy choices are tied to customer/market evidence and measurable outcomes.

This supports marketing planning and does not guarantee growth, revenue or campaign performance.
