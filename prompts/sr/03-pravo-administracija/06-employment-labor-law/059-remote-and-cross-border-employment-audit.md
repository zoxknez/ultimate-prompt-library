# AUDIT UDALJENOG I PREKOGRANIČNOG RADA

Glavni cilj:

> Sprovedite rigoroznu analizu za "Audit udaljenog i prekograničnog rada" koja polazi od stvarnog radnog odnosa, važećeg lokalnog prava, kolektivnih instrumenata i dokumentovanih činjenica, bez pretpostavke da naziv ugovora određuje pravni status.

ILO standarde koristite kao međunarodni referentni okvir kada su relevantni, ali uvek proverite ratifikaciju, implementaciju i konkretno domaće pravo. Preporuke i konvencije ne tretirajte automatski kao neposredno primenjivo domaće pravo.

## 1. ULAZNI GATE

- jurisdikciju rada, poslodavca i zaposlenog
- pravni status i stvarni način rada
- ugovor, anekse, kolektivne ugovore i politike
- radno vreme, lokaciju, reporting line i kontrolu rada
- naknade, beneficije, odsustva i troškove
- performance / discipline istoriju
- imigracioni i work-authorization status gde je relevantan
- predstavnike zaposlenih / sindikat / works council
- ključne datume i procesne rokove
- evidenciju komunikacije, odluka i saglasnosti

## 2. SPECIJALIZOVANI TOK

- Audit remote and cross-border work for employment status, mandatory local protections, payroll, tax/social-security dependencies, immigration, privacy and permanent-establishment flags.
- prinudne minimalne standarde i zaštite
- classification test zasnovan na činjenicama, ne etiketi
- jednako postupanje i zabranu odmazde
- proceduralnu pravičnost i dokumentovanost
- notice, consultation i representation prava
- radno vreme, odmor, leave i pay obaveze
- privacy i monitoring granice
- health & safety dužnosti
- collective-bargaining i representation obaveze
- prekogranične mandatory-law konflikte

## 3. STVARNE ČINJENICE NASPRAM PAPIRA

For each material issue capture:
```text
Worker / role:
Documented term:
Actual practice:
Controlling legal test:
Mandatory or waivable?:
Collective-agreement impact:
Evidence:
Comparator / consistency check:
Worker-rights impact:
Employer risk:
Deadline / process:
Verification status:
```

Never conclude status, misconduct, performance failure or lawful termination solely from a job title, contract label, manager assertion or HR-system field.

## 4. PRAVNI GATE

Verify:
- current labour / employment legislation and implementing rules
- applicable collective agreements and workplace-level instruments
- regulator / labour inspectorate or court authority where relevant
- mandatory local rules that override contract choice
- statutory and contractual deadlines
- protected-status, leave, representation or retaliation issues
- any cross-border conflict-of-laws, immigration or social-security dependency

Distinguish binding law, collective obligations, policy, guidance and good practice.

## 5. RIZICI I FAILURE MODES

Actively test:
- contract wording inconsistent with actual practice
- misclassification
- unlawful deduction or unpaid entitlement
- inconsistent discipline or comparator treatment
- retaliation risk
- defective investigation or lack of opportunity to respond
- missing consultation or representation step
- termination reason unsupported by contemporaneous evidence
- remote-work arrangement triggering foreign mandatory rules
- immigration / work authorization lapse
- policy that conflicts with contract or collective agreement
- health and safety responsibility gaps

Severity:
P0 - immediate illegality, status, authorization or deadline defect capable of invalidating action
P1 - material dismissal, discrimination, wage, classification or collective-rights exposure
P2 - significant process, policy or evidence weakness
P3 - correctable inconsistency or record gap
P4 - workforce-governance hardening

## 8. OBAVEZNI OUTPUT

1. Izvršni employment-risk sažetak.
2. Mapu stvarnog radnog odnosa i relevantnih dokumenata.
3. Primenjivi pravni i kolektivni okvir.
4. Nalaze P0-P4.
5. Obavezne matrice.
6. Rokove, notice i consultation zahteve.
7. Nedostajuće dokaze i dokumentaciju.
8. Remediation / process plan.
9. Prekogranične zavisnosti.
10. Završnu proveru pravičnosti, zakonitosti i dokazivosti.

End with **Provera integriteta radnog odnosa** confirming that conclusions follow verified law and actual facts, not labels or assumptions.

Ovaj prompt pomaže pravnom/HR istraživanju i pripremi. Ne zamenjuje savet kvalifikovanog pravnika za radno pravo u relevantnoj jurisdikciji.
