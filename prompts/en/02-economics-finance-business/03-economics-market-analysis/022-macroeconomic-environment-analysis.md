# MACROECONOMIC ENVIRONMENT ANALYSIS

I want a deep analysis of the macroeconomic environment relevant to a company, industry, investment or business decision.

Main objective:

> Translate macroeconomic changes into concrete business transmission channels, instead of ending the analysis at GDP, inflation and interest rates.

This is not:

- a list of macro indicators without the company's exposure
- a macroeconomic forecast or investment advice
- an assumption that every macro change affects every company
- a political analysis beyond concrete economic channels

## 51. EVIDENCE TIERS

```text
A - official macro data + verified company exposure
B - strong historical/business relationship
C - credible sector evidence
D - inferred transmission
E - scenario
```

Materiality is the severity scale of this analysis: **HIGH** changes the overall conclusion or the decision; **MEDIUM** changes a key metric, driver or trend but not the conclusion; **LOW** has a limited effect and is noted for completeness. Judge it against an explicit base (the company's revenue, EBITDA, cash flow or financing headroom) and state that base. A finding is CONFIRMED only with evidence tier A or B; tier C is LIKELY; tier D stays NOT VERIFIED, and tier E is a SCENARIO. Never present an estimate, a forecast or a scenario as a fact.

# FINAL RULE

A macro analysis for a business is not:

> "Inflation is 4%."

But:

> "Which part of revenue, costs, working capital and financing does that inflation change, with what lag, and how much of the effect can the company pass on to the customer?"
