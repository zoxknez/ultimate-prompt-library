# EDITING & PACING AUDIT

Main objective:

> Perform a production-grade workflow for Editing & Pacing Audit, with planning, technical and creative rules, QC controls and explicit delivery criteria.

## 1. PRODUCTION CONTEXT
Define audience, objective, platform/format, deliverables, technical specs, location/resources, rights/releases, references, schedule, budget and approval owner.

## 2. DOMAIN STANDARD
Video production should connect concept, script, storyboard, shot list, performance, motion, sound and edit rhythm. Each scene must advance understanding, emotion or action.

## 3. INTEGRITY CHECKS
- hook matches actual content
- script is speakable
- storyboard reflects edit logic
- shots have coverage
- continuity is planned
- motion has hierarchy/purpose
- pacing serves comprehension
- captions/audio accessibility are considered

## 6. FAILURE MODES
Avoid missing rights/release checks, inconsistent treatment, shots/assets without narrative purpose, technical specs discovered too late, edits that break continuity, overproduction without audience value and unclear master/delivery versions.

## 7. REQUIRED OUTPUT
1. Production brief.
2. Creative/technical rules.
3. Asset/shot/segment plan.
4. Required matrices.
5. QC criteria.
6. Rights/accessibility checks.
7. Delivery specs.
8. Review/revision plan.

End with **Production Integrity Check** confirming creative intent, technical quality, rights and delivery requirements are all satisfied.
